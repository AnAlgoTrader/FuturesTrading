ES Intraday Dashboard — Data Specification
Overview

The dashboard has 13 panels organized into 4 rows. Each panel below lists its fields, the data required, and notes for the developer. All price fields are in ES futures price space (not SPX cash), unless noted. The developer should confirm symbol conventions with the chosen data provider, as names vary

.
Data Source Recommendations

    Options/GEX data: CBOE DataShop (SPX/SPXW chains), Interactive Brokers API, or a commercial GEX provider. Real-time GEX requires subscribing to all call/put strikes in the chain

.

Market internals: DTN IQFeed carries 700+ breadth indicators including TICK and TRIN, updating every second

.

FedWatch probabilities: CME Group publishes the methodology; developer can either scrape the FedWatch tool or replicate the calculation from ZQ futures prices

.

VIX futures: Cboe Futures Exchange (CFE) market data, available via most futures data feeds

    .

Row 1
Panel 1: Dealer Gamma Regime
Field	Description	Data Needed
Regime status	"NEGATIVE GAMMA" or "POSITIVE GAMMA"	Derived from Net GEX sign
Regime description	Text explaining expected market behavior	Static mapping based on regime status
Put Wall price	Strike with largest negative GEX concentration	From GEX calculation, lowest strike with significant net put gamma
Gamma Flip price	Price where net GEX crosses zero	Interpolate between strikes where cumulative GEX changes sign
Call Wall price	Strike with largest positive GEX concentration	From GEX calculation, highest strike with significant net call gamma
Price bar marker position	Visual position of current spot between walls	Normalize: (spot − put_wall) / (call_wall − put_wall)
SPX spot	Current SPX cash index price	Real-time SPX quote
Distance above/below flip	Points between spot and flip level	spot − flip
0DTE weight	% of total GEX coming from same-day expiry	0DTE_GEX / total_GEX
Net GEX (0DTE-weighted)	Monetary gamma exposure per 1% move	See GEX formula below
Regime change probability (60m)	Estimated odds of regime flip in next hour	Proprietary model; can start with a simple heuristic based on distance to flip and time decay

GEX calculation: The standard formula is:
text

Net GEX(K) = (Gamma_call × OI_call) − (Gamma_put × OI_put)
Net GEX monetary(K) = Net GEX(K) × Contract_Multiplier × Spot_Price² × 0.01

For 0DTE, filter the options chain to contracts expiring the current day. Use real-time or near-real-time data if possible — end-of-day OI misses intraday positioning shifts

.
Panel 2: 0DTE Level Ladder
Field	Description	Data Needed
Level price	Strike price	From GEX calculation, all strikes with significant GEX
Level label	"Call Wall", "Put Wall", "Gamma Flip", etc.	Classification based on GEX sign and magnitude
GEX value	Absolute GEX at that strike	abs(Net GEX(K)) in billions
Row type	Visual styling (resistance/support/flip/spot)	Derived: above spot = resistance, below = support

Sorting: Descending price order. Insert the current spot price as a row between the nearest strikes. If the spot is within 2 points of a wall, consider merging them visually.
Panel 3: Flow Pressure
Field	Description	Data Needed
Charm direction	"Selling pressure" or "Buying pressure"	Second-order Greek: dDelta/dTime. Calculate from 0DTE chain: sum of charm across all strikes weighted by OI
Vanna direction	"Mild bid" or "Mild offer"	Second-order Greek: dDelta/dVol. Sum across 0DTE chain
Net drift bias	Combined directional bias	Heuristic: sum of charm + vanna + recent delta flow
0DTE delta flow (15m)	Net dealer delta change from flow	Track changes in OI × delta over rolling 15-minute window
Session phase	"Opening", "Midday chop", "Power hour"	Time-based: 9:30–10:30 = opening, 10:30–15:00 = midday, 15:00–16:00 = power hour

Developer note: Charm and Vanna are not directly observable; they require a full options chain with Greeks. The calculation uses Black-Scholes or a similar model

.
Row 2
Panel 4: Expected Move
Field	Description	Data Needed
0DTE straddle (ATM)	Price of ATM call + ATM put for today's expiry	Options chain: find strike nearest spot, get call and put last/mid prices
Move consumed	% of expected move already realized	abs(spot − open) / (straddle_price)
Remaining implied	Expected move still available	straddle_price − abs(spot − open)
Realized / Implied	Realized volatility divided by implied	Calculate realized vol from 1-min returns; compare to ATM IV
Session range	Today's high and low	Track intraday SPX or ES high/low
Bar fill width	Visual of move consumed	min(100, move_consumed_pct)
Open price	Session open	First print at 9:30 ET
Target price	Upper bound of expected move	open + straddle_price

Expected move source: The ATM straddle price is the simplest proxy. More sophisticated versions use the 0DTE options chain to build an implied distribution

.
Panel 5: Volatility Structure
Field	Description	Data Needed
VIX spot	Current VIX index level	Real-time VIX quote
VIX change	% change from prior close	(spot − prev_close) / prev_close
VIX M1 / M2	Front and second month VIX futures prices	VIX futures quotes from CFE
Term structure	"Contango" or "Backwardation"	M1 < M2 = contango, M1 > M2 = backwardation
Roll yield	Implied roll return	(M1 − M2) / M2
VVIX	Volatility of VIX	Real-time VVIX quote (Cboe)
IV rank (30d)	Where current IV sits vs. last 30 days	(current_IV − 30d_low) / (30d_high − 30d_low)
Panel 6: Macro Risk
Field	Description	Data Needed
Event name	"FOMC Statement", "CPI Release", etc.	Static calendar (hardcode for now, or scrape from a calendar source)
Time until event	"2d 4h" format	event_datetime − now
FedWatch: hold	Implied probability of no rate change	From CME FedWatch tool or replicate from ZQ futures
FedWatch: cut 25bp	Implied probability of 25bp cut	Same source

FedWatch methodology: The tool uses 30-Day Fed Funds futures (ZQ) prices to derive the probability tree. The core formula for a single meeting:
text

P(Hike) = [EFFR(end of month) − EFFR(start of month)] / 25bp

Where EFFR is derived from the ZQ futures price: EFFR_avg = 100 − ZQ_price

.
Row 3
Panel 7: Market Internals
Field	Description	Data Needed
NYSE TICK	Net up-ticks vs down-ticks across NYSE	Real-time TICK feed; updates every second
TRIN	Arms Index	Real-time TRIN feed; ratio of advancing/declining volume to advancing/declining issues
ADD	Advancing minus declining issues	Real-time breadth feed
UVOL / DVOL	Up-volume vs down-volume ratio	Real-time breadth feed
Breadth divergence	"Watch" or "Clear"	Heuristic: compare ADD trend to ES price trend
ES vs. cash premium	Futures minus cash basis	ES_price − SPX_price
Provider note: Confirm symbol names for each internal with your data provider before building. IQFeed carries these as market stats . Missing data should not block the dashboard — show "N/A" gracefully

.
Panel 8: Session Phase
Field	Description	Data Needed
Current window	Same as Flow Panel	Time-based calculation
Time to power hour	Countdown	15:00 ET − now
0DTE decay (θ)	"Accelerating", "Steady", "Minimal"	Heuristic: time remaining until expiry
Charm peak window	"15:00–16:00"	Static: last hour of trading
Pinning risk	"Low (neg γ)", "High (pos γ)"	Based on regime status + distance to max GEX strike
Panel 9: Regime Triggers
Field	Description	Data Needed
Flip reclaim (bullish)	Price level that would flip to positive gamma	gamma_flip + 1 tick
Flip loss (bearish)	Price level that would flip to negative gamma	gamma_flip − 1 tick
Call wall break	Price that breaks through call wall	call_wall + 1 tick
Put wall break	Price that breaks through put wall	put_wall − 1 tick
Vol trigger (VIX)	VIX level that signals regime change	Heuristic: VIX > 16.5 or 20-day high
Invalidation	Text describing what invalidates the current read	Static or rule-based

Developer note: These are alert thresholds. The dashboard should ideally support audio/visual alerts when any of these are crossed.
Row 4: Scenario Read (Full Width)
Field	Description	Data Needed
Scenario label	"↓ Break below X", "→ Hold range", "↑ Break above Y"	Static text with dynamic price levels inserted
Scenario description	Text explaining what happens in each case	Static text, possibly with dynamic levels

This panel is a synthesis layer. The developer can hardcode the text for v1. In v2, you could generate it dynamically based on regime status, distance to walls, and flow direction.
Data Frequency Summary
Panel	Update Frequency	Latency Sensitivity
Regime	Every 1–5 min	High — flip cross matters
Level Ladder	Every 1–5 min	High
Flow	Every 1–5 min	Medium
Expected Move	Every 1 min	Medium
Volatility	Every 1 min	Low
Macro Risk	Every 1 min (for countdown)	Low
Internals	Every 1 sec	High (for TICK)
Session Phase	Every 1 min	Low
Regime Triggers	Every 1 min	High (for alerts)
Minimum Viable Data Set

If the developer needs to start with a subset, prioritize:

    GEX calculation inputs: SPX/SPXW options chain (strikes, OI, volume, Greeks, expiry) — this powers Regime, Ladder, and Triggers

    VIX futures: Front two months

    ZQ futures: For FedWatch

    Real-time ES and SPX quotes: For basis and spot

Market internals and flow metrics (Charm, Vanna) are valuable but can be added in phase 2.
