ES/MES Momentum Strategy
=========================

DATA
- 1-minute candles built from tick-level mid-price (bid+ask)/2.

ENTRY SIGNAL
- Compute the 1-minute return: log(close[t] / close[t-1]).
- Compute the rolling standard deviation of 1-minute returns.
- If abs(return) >= 2.0 * stdev, enter in the SAME direction as the bar.
  - Positive spike -> go LONG.
  - Negative spike -> go SHORT.

STOPS (time-scaled)
- 1-min return stdev is the base unit.
- Initial stop distance (points) = 2.0 * stdev_bps * sqrt(hold_bars) * price / 10000.
- Trail activation profit        = 0.75 * stdev_bps * sqrt(hold_bars) * price / 10000
  (= 50% of the legacy 1.5x target distance, kept as the activation scale).
- Trail distance (points)        = 1.0 * stdev_bps * sqrt(hold_bars) * price / 10000
  (= 50% of the initial stop distance).

HOLD & EXIT
- Hold for up to 50 bars (~50 minutes).
- Exit conditions checked on every new 1-minute bar:
  - If price reverses through the current stop -> exit.
  - If 50 bars elapsed -> exit at market.
- No take-profit order (removed Aug 2026): winners run until trailed out or timeout.

TRAILING STOP
- Track best price reached since entry (highest high for long, lowest low for short).
- Once profit reaches the activation level:
  - Tighten stop to trail the trail distance behind the best price.
  - Ratchet-only: stop moves with new highs/lows, never loosens.
  - If price reverses through trailing stop -> exit (locks in remaining profit).

SESSION BOUNDARY
- Close any open position at the end of the RTH session.
- Do not enter on the first bar of a new session.

DIRECTION
- Trade both long and short signals.

RISK
- ES point value: $50/point.
- MES point value: $5/point.
- Spread cost: ~$12.50/round trip (ES), ~$1.25 (MES).
- Subtract spread cost from every trade P&L.

BACKTEST RESULTS (IBKR 1-min data, Aug 2025 - Aug 2026)
- Train: $40,544 P&L, 45.3% WR, 10.4 Sharpe, $1,265 max DD, 1,438 trades.
- Test:  $197,356 P&L, 79.6% WR, 43.3 Sharpe, $215 max DD, 1,905 trades.

TAKE-PROFIT COMPARISON (Aug 2026, same winning config with and without TP order)
- TP + Trail: Train $40,544 (Sharpe 10.4, DD $1,265); Test $197,356 (Sharpe 43.4, DD $215).
- Trail only: Train $156,811 (Sharpe 20.6, DD $438); Test $210,003 (Sharpe 45.0, DD $173).
- Decision: drop the take-profit order; trade with the trailing stop only.
