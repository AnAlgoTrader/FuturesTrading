TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: ES FUTURES CUSTOM OHLCV INDICATOR

1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVE

The objective of this indicator is to synthesize Price Action, Market Structure,
Session Reference Levels, and Volume Spread Analysis (VSA) into an automated
visual overlay for trading ES futures using standard 1-minute to 15-minute
OHLCV candlestick data.

VISUAL THEME REQUIREMENT:
- All baseline price candles MUST default to neutral GRAY (body, wicks, borders).
- Specific colors are assigned ONLY to candles that trigger actionable VSA or
  Market Structure signals to highlight high-conviction events instantly.

2. CORE MODULES & CALCULATION LOGIC

MODULE A: SESSION REFERENCE LEVELS (AUTOMATED PLOT)

* Calculate and plot horizontal line levels based on session times (ET):
  1. Regular Trading Hours (RTH): 09:30 AM - 04:15 PM ET
  2. Extended Trading Hours (ETH / Overnight): 06:00 PM - 09:30 AM ET

* Specific Levels to Track & Plot:
  * Previous Day High (PDH), Previous Day Low (PDL), Previous Day Close (PDC)
  * Overnight High (ONH) and Overnight Low (ONL) [Defined from 18:00 to 09:30 ET]
  * Initial Balance High (IBH) and Low (IBL) [Defined from 09:30 to 10:30 ET]

* Display Rules: Extend lines horizontally across the current RTH session until broken.

MODULE B: CANDLE COLORING & VSA SIGNAL LOGIC

Default State: All standard/neutral bars = Neutral Gray (Body, Wick, Border).

* Signal 1: BULLISH ABSORPTION / STOPPING VOLUME (Color: GREEN / LIME)
  * Logic: High/Ultra-High Volume (>= 1.5x 20-bar Volume SMA) AND
           Narrow Spread (Spread <= 0.5x 20-bar Avg Spread) AND
           (Occurs near session lows OR lower wick >= 50% of bar range).
  * Meaning: Heavy institutional buying absorbing aggressive selling.

* Signal 2: BEARISH ABSORPTION / TOPPING VOLUME (Color: RED / MAGENTA)
  * Logic: High/Ultra-High Volume (>= 1.5x 20-bar Volume SMA) AND
           Narrow Spread (Spread <= 0.5x 20-bar Avg Spread) AND
           (Occurs near session highs OR upper wick >= 50% of bar range).
  * Meaning: Heavy institutional selling absorbing aggressive buying.

* Signal 3: BULLISH LIQUIDITY SWEEP (Color: CYAN / LIGHT BLUE)
  * Logic: Low breaches a key support (PDL, ONL, or Swing Low), but candle Close
           returns ABOVE that level within the same bar.
  * Meaning: Stop-run / liquidity raid below support followed by instant rejection.

* Signal 4: BEARISH LIQUIDITY SWEEP (Color: ORANGE)
  * Logic: High breaches a key resistance (PDH, ONH, or Swing High), but candle Close
           returns BELOW that level within the same bar.
  * Meaning: Stop-run / liquidity raid above resistance followed by instant rejection.

* Signal 5: ULTRA-HIGH VOLUME INITIATION / EXPANSION (Color: YELLOW)
  * Logic: Volume >= 2.0x 20-bar Volume SMA AND Spread >= 1.5x 20-bar Avg Spread.
  * Meaning: Strong directional push / institutional initiative movement.

* Signal Priority (In case multiple conditions meet):
  Sweep (Cyan/Orange) > Absorption (Green/Red) > Volume Expansion (Yellow) > Gray.

MODULE C: MARKET STRUCTURE SHIFTS (MSS)

* Swing High/Low Detection:
  * Defined using a configurable N-bar pivot parameter (Default: Pivot Left = 3, Pivot Right = 3).

* Structure Lines:
  * Draw temporary horizontal lines across active Swing Highs/Lows and key reference levels.

MODULE D: ANCHORED VOLUME-WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE (VWAP)

* Logic: Standard intraday VWAP formula:
  VWAP = Sum(Typical Price * Volume) / Sum(Volume)
  Where Typical Price = (High + Low + Close) / 3

* Reset Condition: Reset at the start of RTH session (09:30 AM ET).

* Standard Deviation Bands:
  * Upper/Lower Band 1: VWAP +/- 1.0 * Standard Deviation
  * Upper/Lower Band 2: VWAP +/- 2.0 * Standard Deviation

3. INPUT PARAMETERS (USER CONFIGURABLE)

1. Session Times (ET):
   * Initial Balance Duration (Default: 60 minutes)

2. Volume Averages & Multipliers:
   * Volume SMA Length (Default: 20)
   * High Volume Multiplier (Default: 1.5)
   * Ultra-High Volume Multiplier (Default: 2.0)
   * Narrow Spread Multiplier (Default: 0.5)

3. Pivot Strength (Default: 3 bars left, 3 bars right)

4. Color Customization Palette:
   * Neutral Bar Color (Default: Gray)
   * Bullish Absorption Color (Default: Green)
   * Bearish Absorption Color (Default: Red)
   * Bullish Sweep Color (Default: Cyan)
   * Bearish Sweep Color (Default: Orange)
   * Expansion Volume Color (Default: Yellow)

4. VISUAL DISPLAY & CHART OUTPUT SUMMARY

* Default Chart State: Clean gray candles over a dark background.
* Signals: Highlighted single candles standing out in bright signal colors (Green, Red, Cyan, Orange, Yellow).
